Delete Your Account

Edutracker – Smart Tuition Management

⚠ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently deleted.

1. What Data Will Be Deleted

When you delete your Edutracker account, the following data is permanently removed from our servers and your device:

Data Type Status
Student Records – Names, phone numbers, notes, class info Deleted
Batch Information – Batch names, schedules, student lists Deleted
Payment History – Fee records, payment status, amounts Deleted
Subscription Requests – UPI payment requests and approval status Deleted
App Settings – Preferences, onboarding status, local configuration Deleted
Firebase Account – Your Google Sign-In authentication account Deleted

2. How to Delete Your Account

Method A: Delete from Within the App (Recommended)

The easiest way to delete your account is directly from the Edutracker app:

  1. Open Edutracker

    Launch the app and make sure you are signed in.

  2. Tap Your Profile Icon

    Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner of the dashboard to open the profile menu.

  3. Tap "Delete Account"

    Scroll down and tap the red "Delete Account" button at the bottom of the profile menu.

  4. Confirm Deletion

    You will be asked to confirm. Tap "Delete Forever" to proceed. You may be asked to re-authenticate with your Google account for security.

  5. Account Deleted

    All your data will be permanently removed and you will be signed out automatically.

Method B: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us. Please include the email address associated with your Edutracker account.

Send a deletion request to:

privacy@pglive.dev

We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation email.

3. What Happens After Deletion

  • Immediate: Your account is disabled and you are signed out.
  • Within 7 days: All Firestore data (students, batches, payments, subscription requests) is permanently deleted from Google Cloud servers.
  • Within 30 days: Your Firebase Authentication account is permanently removed. Backup copies in our disaster recovery systems are also purged.
  • Local data: All data stored locally on your device (Hive database) is cleared immediately upon deletion.

4. Important Notes

  • Account deletion is irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
  • If you have an active premium subscription, it will be cancelled upon account deletion. No refunds are provided for unused subscription time.
  • You may need to re-authenticate with your Google account before deletion as a security measure.
  • If you are using a shared device, please ensure you sign out after deletion to prevent unauthorized access.
